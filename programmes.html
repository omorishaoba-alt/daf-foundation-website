Our Programmes

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation implements programmes designed to improve lives, strengthen communities and create sustainable opportunities across Nigeria.

Community Development

Healthy communities are the foundation of national development.

  • Rural infrastructure support
  • Water and sanitation projects
  • Youth empowerment
  • Women empowerment
  • Agricultural support
  • Community leadership development
  • Skills acquisition programmes
  • Poverty reduction initiatives
  • Community advocacy
  • Economic empowerment

Our objective is to build self-reliant communities capable of sustaining their own development.

Health Care Support

Good health is essential for human development.

  • Medical outreach
  • Free health screening
  • Maternal and child healthcare
  • Health education
  • Preventive healthcare campaigns
  • Eye care programmes
  • Blood pressure and diabetes screening
  • Mental health awareness

2027–2028 Strategic Work Programme

Our vision is to build stronger, healthier and more prosperous communities through sustainable interventions that improve lives, promote education, protect vulnerable people and create opportunities for future generations.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities through education, healthcare, youth development, anti-human trafficking advocacy, research support, humanitarian assistance and sustainable community development.

Year One (2027)

Education Development Programme

  • Award scholarships to outstanding and indigent students.
  • Donate books, computers and educational materials to schools.
  • Organise free holiday coaching classes.
  • Sponsor teacher development workshops.
  • Launch a "Back to School" support programme.

Community Healthcare Initiative

  • Conduct quarterly free medical outreaches.
  • Provide free eye screening and distribution of reading glasses.
  • Organise hypertension and diabetes screening.
  • Maternal and child healthcare awareness campaigns.
  • Blood donation drives.

Youth Empowerment Programme

  • Skills acquisition training.
  • ICT and digital literacy classes.
  • Entrepreneurship development workshops.
  • Small business grants and starter packs.
  • Career guidance and mentorship.

Women Empowerment Programme

  • Vocational training.
  • Small business support grants.
  • Financial literacy training.
  • Leadership development for women.

Anti-Human Trafficking Campaign

  • School awareness campaigns.
  • Community sensitisation programmes.
  • Youth education on safe migration.
  • Collaboration with security agencies and civil society organisations.

Humanitarian Services

  • Food distribution to vulnerable families.
  • Support for widows and the elderly.
  • Emergency relief interventions.
  • Assistance for persons living with disabilities.

Research and Policy Development

  • Small research grants.
  • Community needs assessments.
  • Publication of annual community development reports.

Expected Impact (2027–2028)

  • Over 5,000 people reached through healthcare interventions.
  • More than 500 students supported with scholarships and educational materials.
  • 2,000 youths and women empowered through skills and entrepreneurship programmes.
  • 100 communities reached with anti-human trafficking awareness.
  • Hundreds of vulnerable families supported through humanitarian assistance.
  • Sustainable partnerships with government agencies, development partners and private organisations.

Partnership

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation welcomes partnerships with government agencies, development organisations, corporate institutions, donor agencies, educational institutions, healthcare providers and individuals who share our vision of creating sustainable and inclusive community development across Nigeria.

Together, we can transform lives, strengthen communities and build a brighter future for generations to come.

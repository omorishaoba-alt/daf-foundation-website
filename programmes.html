Bill Asekhame Youth Leadership Development Roadmap (2027–2030)

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is committed to nurturing young leaders who are passionate about service, compassion and sustainable community development.

This structured leadership development journey is designed to equip young people with the knowledge, skills and practical experience required to become respected humanitarian and community leaders.

Year One (Age 14–15)

Foundation of Service — Learning, Character Building and Volunteerism.

Year Two (Age 15–16)

Emerging Leadership — Leading Through Action.

Year Three (Age 16–17)

National Youth Leadership — Creating Lasting Community Impact.

Year Four (Age 17–18)

Global Youth Leadership — Serving Communities Beyond Borders.

About DAF Foundation

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit humanitarian and community development organisation committed to transforming lives through sustainable social interventions across Nigeria.

We work with communities, educational institutions, healthcare providers, development partners and government agencies to create measurable impact and lasting change.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities and protection from exploitation while promoting sustainable community development.

Our Mission

Improving lives through strategic interventions in education, healthcare, humanitarian support, community development, research and anti-human trafficking advocacy.

Founder & President

Hon. Daniel Asekhame

Hon. Daniel Asekhame is a distinguished public servant, philanthropist, businessman and humanitarian dedicated to improving lives through community development, education, healthcare and sustainable social impact.

He served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government and currently provides strategic leadership for the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, advancing programmes that strengthen communities across Nigeria.

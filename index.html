Welcome to DAF Foundation

Empowering People. Strengthening Communities. Building Hope.

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organisation dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

We believe sustainable development begins with empowered people. Through strategic partnerships, compassion, accountability and innovation, we deliver programmes that create measurable and lasting impact in education, healthcare, youth empowerment, humanitarian assistance, anti-human trafficking advocacy and research funding.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and protection from exploitation, while promoting sustainable community development and supporting research that transforms lives.

Our Mission

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is committed to improving lives through strategic interventions in community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian support and research funding. We partner with communities, government agencies, educational institutions and development organisations to create lasting solutions that empower individuals and strengthen communities.

About DAF Foundation

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organisation dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programmes focus on providing hope, opportunities, education, healthcare and protection to vulnerable individuals while promoting inclusive growth within communities.

We work with community leaders, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, development partners, volunteers and private organisations to deliver measurable and sustainable impact.

Our approach combines compassion, professionalism, accountability, transparency and innovation to ensure that every intervention produces meaningful and lasting change.

Our Core Values

The values that guide every programme, partnership and intervention.

Integrity

Integrity is the foundation of everything we do at the Daniel Asekhame Foundation. We are committed to honesty, fairness, ethical conduct and respect in all our actions and decisions. We uphold the highest standards of professionalism, ensuring that our programmes and partnerships are guided by transparency, responsibility and a genuine commitment to serving humanity.

Accountability

We are committed to managing all resources with honesty, integrity and transparency. Every donation, partnership and contribution entrusted to us is used responsibly to achieve meaningful and measurable impact in the communities we serve.

Compassion

Compassion is at the heart of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation. We are driven by a genuine concern for the well-being of others and a commitment to serving with kindness, empathy and dignity.

Transparency

Transparency is a guiding principle of the Foundation. We are committed to openness in our operations, decision-making and use of resources while maintaining public confidence through honest communication and responsible reporting.

We believe everyone deserves equal opportunities to thrive regardless of age, gender, disability, ethnicity, religion or socio-economic background.

Service to Humanity

Respect for Human Dignity

Every individual deserves to be treated with dignity, respect and compassion. We promote equality, inclusion and respect in every programme.

Innovation

We embrace creative ideas, modern technology and evidence-based solutions to address evolving community challenges while creating sustainable impact.

Hon. Daniel Asekhame

Founder & President

Hon. Daniel Asekhame is a distinguished public servant, community development advocate, philanthropist, businessman and humanitarian dedicated to improving the welfare of individuals and communities.

A native of Owan West Local Government Area of Edo State, he served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government, where he promoted people-oriented programmes, strengthened community participation and supported sustainable development initiatives.

He holds a B.Sc. degree from Ambrose Alli University, an M.Sc. degree from the University of Benin, and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

He is Chief Executive Officer of Dase Kingsgate Ltd and Landlion Haulage Ltd, and has received more than thirty awards and honours, including the prestigious Rotary International Paul Harris Fellow (PHF) Award.

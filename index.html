About the Foundation

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is a humanitarian organisation committed to improving lives, strengthening communities and creating sustainable opportunities through compassion, service and strategic partnerships.

About Us

The Foundation exists to improve the quality of life of vulnerable individuals and communities through impactful programmes in education, healthcare, youth empowerment, women empowerment, humanitarian assistance, anti-human trafficking advocacy, research support and community development.

Working alongside government agencies, development partners, corporate organisations, civil society and local communities, the Foundation seeks practical and sustainable solutions that produce measurable social impact.

Our Vision

To build resilient, inclusive and prosperous communities where every individual has access to opportunities that promote dignity, hope, wellbeing and sustainable development.

Our Mission

To transform lives through innovative humanitarian programmes, strategic partnerships and community-driven initiatives that empower people, strengthen families and foster lasting social and economic development.

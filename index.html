Welcome to Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation)

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organization dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programs focus on providing hope, opportunities, education, healthcare, and protection to vulnerable individuals while promoting inclusive growth within communities.

We work with community leaders, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, development partners, volunteers, and private organizations to deliver measurable and sustainable impact.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and protection from exploitation, while promoting sustainable community development and supporting research that transforms lives.

Our Mission

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is committed to improving lives through strategic interventions in community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian support, and research funding. We partner with communities, government agencies, educational institutions, and development organizations to create lasting solutions that empower individuals and strengthen communities.

Our Areas of Focus

Community Development

Healthy communities are the foundation of national development. Our community development initiatives include rural infrastructure support, water and sanitation projects, youth empowerment, women empowerment, agricultural support, community leadership development, skills acquisition programmes, poverty reduction initiatives, community advocacy and economic empowerment.

Health Care Support

Good health is essential for human development. Our healthcare programmes include medical outreach, free health screening, maternal and child healthcare, health education, preventive healthcare campaigns, eye care programmes, blood pressure and diabetes screening, mental health awareness, medical assistance for vulnerable persons and support for persons living with disabilities.

Education

Education remains the greatest investment in human capital. Our educational interventions include scholarships, school supplies, teacher support programmes, educational grants, school infrastructure support, digital learning initiatives, adult literacy programmes, career development, mentorship and leadership training.

Combating Human Trafficking

DAF Foundation is committed to public awareness campaigns, prevention programmes, youth education, community sensitisation, victim support, rehabilitation initiatives, reintegration support, advocacy, collaboration with law enforcement agencies and policy engagement.

Research Funding

DAF Foundation supports research that provides practical solutions to societal challenges in public health, education, community development, agriculture, youth development, economic empowerment, environmental sustainability, technology, social policy and governance.

Why DAF Foundation?

We are committed to creating measurable social impact through sustainable programmes that address the root causes of poverty, inequality, illiteracy, poor healthcare, and exploitation.

Our strength lies in collaboration, transparency, accountability, and a deep commitment to serving humanity.

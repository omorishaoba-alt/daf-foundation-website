About Daniel Asekhame Foundation

Building Hope • Empowering People • Strengthening Communities

Who We Are

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organisation dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programmes focus on providing hope, opportunities, education, healthcare and protection to vulnerable individuals while promoting inclusive growth within communities.

We work with community leaders, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, development partners, volunteers and private organisations to deliver measurable and sustainable impact.

Our approach combines compassion, professionalism, accountability, transparency and innovation to ensure that every intervention produces meaningful and lasting change.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and protection from exploitation, while promoting sustainable community development and supporting research that transforms lives.

Our Mission

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is committed to improving lives through strategic interventions in community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian support and research funding.

Our Core Values

These values guide every decision, partnership and programme of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation.

Integrity

Integrity is the foundation of everything we do at the Daniel Asekhame Foundation. We are committed to honesty, fairness, ethical conduct and respect in all our actions and decisions. We uphold the highest standards of professionalism, ensuring that our programmes and partnerships are guided by transparency, responsibility and a genuine commitment to serving humanity.

Accountability

We are committed to managing all resources with honesty, integrity and transparency. Every donation, partnership and contribution entrusted to us is used responsibly to achieve meaningful and measurable impact in the communities we serve.

Compassion

Compassion is at the heart of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation. We are driven by a genuine concern for the well-being of others and a commitment to serving with kindness, empathy and dignity.

Transparency

We are committed to openness in our operations, decision-making and use of resources. Through honest communication and responsible reporting, we strengthen trust with beneficiaries, partners and supporters.

Excellence

We pursue excellence through innovation, professionalism and continuous improvement, ensuring every programme delivers sustainable and measurable impact.

Inclusion

We believe everyone deserves equal opportunities regardless of age, gender, disability, ethnicity, religion or socio-economic background. Diversity is respected and every voice is valued.

Service to Humanity

Service to humanity is the driving force behind the Foundation. We are committed to improving lives through selfless service, compassion and sustainable community development.

Respect for Human Dignity

Every individual deserves to be treated with dignity, compassion and respect. We promote equality, inclusion and empowerment in every intervention.

Innovation

We embrace creative ideas, modern technology and evidence-based solutions to address evolving community challenges and deliver meaningful long-term results.

Sustainable Development

We implement programmes that strengthen education, healthcare, youth empowerment, environmental stewardship and community development while creating lasting social and economic impact.

Hon. Daniel Asekhame

Founder & President

Hon. Daniel Asekhame is a distinguished public servant, community development advocate, philanthropist, businessman and humanitarian whose life has been dedicated to improving the welfare of individuals and communities.

A native of Owan West Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria, he has devoted much of his career to promoting good governance, community empowerment, youth development and social inclusion.

He served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government, first by appointment for one term and subsequently by election for two terms. During his tenure he promoted people-oriented programmes, strengthened community participation in governance and supported sustainable local development.

Hon. Asekhame holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree from Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma, a Master of Science (M.Sc.) degree from the University of Benin and is a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

He is the Chief Executive Officer of Dase Kingsgate Ltd and Landlion Haulage Ltd. His business experience has reinforced his belief that entrepreneurship, economic empowerment and job creation are essential for reducing poverty and promoting sustainable development.

Our Commitment

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is committed to improving lives through education, healthcare, humanitarian assistance, youth and women empowerment, anti-human trafficking advocacy, research funding and sustainable community development.

We believe lasting transformation is achieved through compassion, integrity, accountability, transparency, innovation and strong partnerships with communities, government agencies, educational institutions, development organisations and the private sector.

Under the leadership of Hon. Daniel Asekhame, the Foundation remains dedicated to building stronger communities, creating equal opportunities and leaving a lasting legacy of service for future generations.

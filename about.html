About DAF

A Foundation Built Around Service

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is committed to creating meaningful and sustainable impact across Nigeria.

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organization dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programmes focus on education, healthcare, humanitarian support, community development, protection from exploitation and research that can improve lives.

We work with communities, institutions, government agencies, development partners, volunteers and private organizations to create solutions that produce meaningful and lasting change.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities and protection from exploitation, while promoting sustainable community development and supporting research that transforms lives.

Our Mission

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is committed to improving lives through strategic interventions in community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian support and research funding.

Leadership

Hon. Daniel Asekhame

Founder & President, Daniel Asekhame Foundation

A Life Dedicated to Service

Hon. Daniel Asekhame is a distinguished public servant, community development advocate, philanthropist, businessman and humanitarian whose life has been dedicated to improving the welfare of individuals and communities.

A native of Owan West Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria, he has devoted much of his career to promoting good governance, community empowerment, youth development and social inclusion.

He served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government, where he was first appointed for one term and subsequently elected for two terms. During his tenure, he promoted people-oriented programmes and strengthened community participation in governance.

Hon. Asekhame holds a Bachelor of Science degree from Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma, and a Master of Science degree from the University of Benin. He is also a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

The Journey Continues

Public Service, Enterprise & Humanity

Beyond politics and public service, Hon. Daniel Asekhame is an accomplished entrepreneur and the Chief Executive Officer of Dase Kingsgate Ltd and Landlion Haulage Ltd.

His business experience has reinforced his belief that economic empowerment, entrepreneurship and job creation are essential tools for reducing poverty and promoting sustainable development.

His passion for sports also earned him recognition as the former Chairman of the Edo State Volleyball Association, where he contributed to the growth of volleyball and youth participation in sports across the state.

In recognition of his service to humanity and community development, Hon. Daniel Asekhame has received more than thirty awards and honours from professional bodies, civic organizations, religious institutions, youth groups and community associations.

He is also a recipient of the prestigious Rotary International Paul Harris Fellow (PHF) Award.

As Founder and President of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, he provides strategic leadership for programmes focused on community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian assistance, youth and women empowerment and research funding.

Hon. Daniel Asekhame believes that true leadership is measured not by positions held but by the number of lives positively impacted. His vision is to build a Foundation that serves as a beacon of hope and leaves a lasting legacy of compassion, service and transformational leadership.

Our Commitment

Accountability

At the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, accountability is one of our core values. We are committed to managing all resources with honesty, integrity and transparency.

Every donation, partnership and contribution entrusted to us is used responsibly to achieve meaningful and measurable impact in the communities we serve.

Through openness, ethical governance and responsible leadership, we strive to earn and sustain the confidence of beneficiaries, partners, donors and stakeholders.

About Us

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organization dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

Who We Are

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programs focus on providing hope, opportunities, education, healthcare, and protection to vulnerable individuals while promoting inclusive growth within communities.

We work with community leaders, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, development partners, volunteers, and private organizations to deliver measurable and sustainable impact.

Our approach combines compassion, professionalism, accountability, transparency, and innovation to ensure that every intervention produces meaningful and lasting change.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and protection from exploitation, while promoting sustainable community development and supporting research that transforms lives.

Our Mission

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is committed to improving lives through strategic interventions in community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian support, and research funding. We partner with communities, government agencies, educational institutions, and development organizations to create lasting solutions that empower individuals and strengthen communities.

About The President

Hon. Daniel Asekhame

Founder & President, Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation)

Hon. Daniel Asekhame is a distinguished public servant, community development advocate, philanthropist, businessman, and humanitarian whose life has been dedicated to improving the welfare of individuals and communities.

Through decades of leadership in public service and private initiatives, he has earned a reputation for integrity, compassion, and a deep commitment to grassroots development.

A native of Owan West Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria, Hon. Asekhame has devoted much of his career to promoting good governance, community empowerment, youth development, and social inclusion.

He served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government, where he was first appointed for one term and subsequently elected for two terms. During his tenure, he promoted people-oriented programmes, strengthened community participation in governance, and supported initiatives aimed at sustainable local development.

Hon. Asekhame holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree from Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma, and a Master of Science (M.Sc.) degree from the University of Benin. He is also a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

Beyond politics and public service, he is an accomplished entrepreneur and the Chief Executive Officer of Dase Kingsgate Ltd and Landlion Haulage Ltd. His business experience has reinforced his belief that economic empowerment, entrepreneurship, and job creation are essential tools for reducing poverty and promoting sustainable development.

His passion for sports also earned him recognition as the former Chairman of the Edo State Volleyball Association, where he contributed to the growth and development of volleyball and youth participation in sports across the state.

In recognition of his outstanding service to humanity and community development, Hon. Daniel Asekhame has received more than thirty awards and honours from professional bodies, civic organizations, religious institutions, youth groups, and community associations.

He is also a recipient of the prestigious Rotary International Paul Harris Fellow (PHF) Award, one of Rotary's highest recognitions for exceptional service to society.

As Founder and President of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation), he provides strategic leadership for programmes focused on community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian assistance, youth and women empowerment, and research funding.

Under his leadership, the Foundation is committed to delivering sustainable interventions that uplift vulnerable populations and strengthen communities across Nigeria.

Hon. Daniel Asekhame firmly believes that true leadership is measured not by positions held but by the number of lives positively impacted.

His vision is to build a Foundation that serves as a beacon of hope, promotes equal opportunities, supports innovation and research, and leaves a lasting legacy of compassion, service, and transformational leadership for future generations.

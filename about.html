About DAF Foundation

Building Hope. Empowering People. Strengthening Communities.

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is built around a simple commitment: to use service, compassion and purposeful action to improve lives.

Who We Are

Daniel Asekhame Foundation

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organization dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programmes focus on providing hope, opportunities, education, healthcare and protection to vulnerable individuals while promoting inclusive growth within communities.

We work with community leaders, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, development partners, volunteers and private organizations to deliver meaningful and sustainable impact.

Our approach combines compassion, professionalism, accountability, transparency and innovation to ensure that every intervention contributes to lasting positive change.

Direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual, regardless of background, has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities and protection from exploitation, while promoting sustainable community development and supporting research that transforms lives.

Our Mission

The Daniel Asekhame Foundation is committed to improving lives through strategic interventions in community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian support and research funding.

We partner with communities, government agencies, educational institutions and development organizations to create lasting solutions that empower individuals and strengthen communities.

The Founder & President

The person at the centre of the Foundation's vision for service to humanity.

Founder & President

Hon. Daniel Asekhame

Public Servant • Community Development Advocate • Philanthropist • Humanitarian

Hon. Daniel Asekhame is a distinguished public servant, community development advocate, philanthropist, businessman and humanitarian whose life has been dedicated to improving the welfare of individuals and communities.

A native of Owan West Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria, Hon. Asekhame has devoted much of his career to promoting good governance, community empowerment, youth development and social inclusion.

Read His Story

He served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government, where he was first appointed for one term and subsequently elected for two terms. During his tenure, he promoted people-oriented programmes, strengthened community participation in governance and supported initiatives aimed at sustainable local development.

Hon. Asekhame holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree from Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma, and a Master of Science (M.Sc.) degree from the University of Benin. He is also a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

Beyond politics and public service, he is an accomplished entrepreneur and the Chief Executive Officer of Dase Kingsgate Ltd and Landlion Haulage Ltd. His business experience has reinforced his belief that economic empowerment, entrepreneurship and job creation are essential tools for reducing poverty and promoting sustainable development.

His passion for sports also earned him recognition as the former Chairman of the Edo State Volleyball Association, where he contributed to the growth and development of volleyball and youth participation in sports across the state.

In recognition of his outstanding service to humanity and community development, Hon. Daniel Asekhame has received more than thirty awards and honours from professional bodies, civic organizations, religious institutions, youth groups and community associations. He is also a recipient of the prestigious Rotary International Paul Harris Fellow (PHF) Award.

As Founder and President of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, he provides strategic leadership for programmes focused on community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian assistance, youth and women empowerment and research funding.

Hon. Daniel Asekhame firmly believes that true leadership is measured not by positions held but by the number of lives positively impacted. His vision is to build a Foundation that serves as a beacon of hope, promotes equal opportunities, supports innovation and research, and leaves a lasting legacy of compassion, service and transformational leadership for future generations.

Our Values

Principles That Guide Our Service

The Foundation's work is guided by principles that place people, dignity, responsibility and meaningful impact at the centre of service.

Integrity

We are committed to honesty, fairness, ethical conduct and respect in all our actions and decisions.

Accountability

We accept responsibility for our actions, programmes and commitments while maintaining transparency with our beneficiaries, partners and communities.

Compassion

We serve with empathy, kindness and genuine concern for the welfare and dignity of others.

Transparency

We believe openness and responsible communication are essential to building lasting trust.

Excellence

We pursue high standards of professionalism, quality, innovation and continuous improvement.

Inclusion

We believe everyone deserves an opportunity to participate, contribute and thrive.

Service to Humanity

Service to humanity is central to our purpose and informs the way we design and deliver our work.

Respect for Human Dignity

Every person deserves dignity, respect and compassionate treatment regardless of background or circumstance.

Innovation

We encourage creative thinking, appropriate technology and evidence-based solutions to social challenges.

Sustainable Development

We seek solutions that create meaningful benefits today while strengthening opportunities for future generations.

Our Commitment

Accountability

At the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, accountability is one of our core values. We are committed to managing all resources with honesty, integrity and transparency.

Our Accountability Commitment

Every donation, partnership and contribution entrusted to us is used responsibly to achieve meaningful and measurable impact in the communities we serve.

We maintain high standards of financial stewardship, ethical governance and responsible leadership to ensure that our beneficiaries, partners, donors and stakeholders can have confidence in our work.

Through openness, responsible management and a commitment to service, we strive to earn and sustain the trust of everyone who supports our mission.

About Daniel Asekhame Foundation

Daniel Asekhame Foundation (DAF Foundation) is a non-profit, humanitarian and community development organization dedicated to transforming lives across Nigeria through impactful social interventions.

The Foundation believes that sustainable development begins with empowered people. Our programmes focus on providing hope, opportunities, education, healthcare and protection to vulnerable individuals while promoting inclusive growth within communities.

We work with community leaders, educational institutions, healthcare providers, government agencies, development partners, volunteers and private organizations to deliver measurable and sustainable impact.

Our approach combines compassion, professionalism, accountability, transparency and innovation to ensure that every intervention produces meaningful and lasting change.

Leadership

The Founder & President

Public Service & Community Leadership

A native of Owan West Local Government Area of Edo State, Nigeria, Hon. Asekhame has devoted much of his career to promoting good governance, community empowerment, youth development and social inclusion.

He served as Chairman of Owan West Local Government, where he was first appointed for one term and subsequently elected for two terms. During his tenure, he promoted people-oriented programmes, strengthened community participation in governance and supported initiatives aimed at sustainable local development.

Education & Professional Development

Hon. Asekhame holds a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degree from Ambrose Alli University, Ekpoma, and a Master of Science (M.Sc.) degree from the University of Benin.

He is also a certified Project Management Professional (PMP).

Entrepreneurship & Economic Empowerment

Beyond politics and public service, he is an accomplished entrepreneur and the Chief Executive Officer of Dase Kingsgate Ltd and Landlion Haulage Ltd.

His business experience has reinforced his belief that economic empowerment, entrepreneurship and job creation are essential tools for reducing poverty and promoting sustainable development.

Sports & Youth Development

His passion for sports also earned him recognition as the former Chairman of the Edo State Volleyball Association, where he contributed to the growth and development of volleyball and youth participation in sports across the state.

Recognition & Service to Humanity

In recognition of his outstanding service to humanity and community development, Hon. Daniel Asekhame has received more than thirty awards and honours from professional bodies, civic organizations, religious institutions, youth groups and community associations.

He is also a recipient of the prestigious Rotary International Paul Harris Fellow (PHF) Award.

His Foundation Leadership

As Founder and President of the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, he provides strategic leadership for programmes focused on community development, healthcare, education, anti-human trafficking advocacy, humanitarian assistance, youth and women empowerment and research funding.

Hon. Daniel Asekhame firmly believes that true leadership is measured not by positions held but by the number of lives positively impacted.

His vision is to build a Foundation that serves as a beacon of hope, promotes equal opportunities, supports innovation and research, and leaves a lasting legacy of compassion, service and transformational leadership for future generations.

Our Commitment

Accountability

At the Daniel Asekhame Foundation, accountability is one of our core values. We are committed to managing all resources with honesty, integrity and transparency.

Every donation, partnership and contribution entrusted to us is used responsibly to achieve meaningful and measurable impact in the communities we serve.

We maintain high standards of financial stewardship, ethical governance and regular reporting to ensure that our beneficiaries, partners, donors and stakeholders have confidence in our work.

Through openness and responsible leadership, we strive to earn and sustain the trust of everyone who supports our mission.
